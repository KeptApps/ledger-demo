How Ledger works

Ledger is a single file that runs in your browser. There is no account, no bank connection and no internet connection needed. Your figures are stored privately by your own browser on this device. They are never sent to us and there is no Ledger cloud account.

Keep the file somewhere permanent. Documents is ideal. Bookmark it or make a desktop shortcut.

Back up now and then. Press Back up and you get a .json file with everything in it. That same file is how you move to a new laptop: open Ledger there and press Restore.

Clearing browsing data can wipe it. That's the one thing to watch for, and a recent backup makes it a non-event.

Safe to spend is your income for the month, minus what you have already spent, minus the bills and subscriptions still to come. Press "How is this worked out?" to see every figure. It is arithmetic on your own numbers, not advice.
